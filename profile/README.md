# Maison de la Simulation

### Computing for science, science for computing

**Maison de la Simulation (MdlS)** is a joint research laboratory of **CEA, CNRS, Université Paris-Saclay and Université de Versailles Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines**, dedicated to advancing computational science and high-performance computing (HPC).

We bring together researchers and research software engineers to develop numerical methods, algorithms and software for large-scale scientific computing, bridging computer science, applied mathematics and scientific applications.

🌐 [Website](https://mdls.fr) · 💼 [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/maison-de-la-simulation) · 💻 [GitHub](https://github.com/maison-de-la-simulation)

## 🔬 Research & expertise

Our activities cover a broad spectrum of computational science:

- **High-performance computing** — Parallel algorithms, performance optimization, heterogeneous architectures and exascale computing.
- **Scientific software engineering** — Portable and sustainable research software, GPU programming, programming models and HPC libraries.
- **Artificial intelligence for science** — Machine learning, scientific foundation models, AI-assisted simulations and data-intensive computing.
- **Numerical methods & simulation** — Applied mathematics, multiphysics modeling, numerical algorithms and scientific applications.
- **Visualization & scientific data** — Large-scale data analysis, interactive visualization and in situ processing.
- **Quantum computing** — Quantum algorithms, quantum simulation and hybrid HPC–quantum workflows.
- **Emerging architectures** — Energy-efficient computing, FPGAs and alternative computing paradigms.

## 🚀 What we do

Maison de la Simulation combines three complementary missions:

**Research:** Advance computational science through interdisciplinary research in algorithms, methods, software and applications.

**Expertise & support:** Help scientific communities develop, optimize and scale their applications on modern HPC infrastructures.

**Training & community:** Share expertise through hands-on training, workshops, hackathons and collaborations with academic and industrial partners.
